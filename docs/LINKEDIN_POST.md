### Main Post Draft

Production outages at 3 AM are stressful enough. But what's worse is watching an engineering team spend hours investigating an incident that was already solved two months ago by someone who rotated off-call.

We call this **Operational Amnesia** — and it costs engineering organizations millions in unnecessary downtime.

To solve this, we built **OpsMind**: an intelligent AI Incident Response Agent with genuine persistent memory, powered by Vectorize Hindsight.

Unlike standard chatbots or naive vector RAG that suffer from session amnesia, OpsMind:
- Remembers past incident timelines, root causes, and verified fixes across shifts.
- Uses 4-way hybrid recall (semantic, exact keyword BM25, graph topology, and temporal context) to match recurring outages in seconds.
- Conducts autonomous causal reflection, clearly separating confirmed root causes from speculative hypotheses.
- Maintains living "Mental Models" of service reliability that continuously improve after every post-mortem.
- Enforces strict Human-in-the-Loop guardrails — requiring explicit SRE sign-off before executing any production remediation command.

In our benchmark demonstration, an outage that previously required hours of troubleshooting was triaged and resolved with a verified runbook in under 2 seconds during a recurrence.
